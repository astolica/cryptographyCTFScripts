RSA Encryption
1. RSA involved modular exponentiation, which is raising a number to a certain power,then taking the remainder of the division by another number.
1a. The function pow(base, exponent, modulus), helps you calculate this
2. In RSA, Modular exponentiation, together with prime facorization, helps create a trapdoor function. Something that is easy to computer in one direction, but hard to reverse unless you have the right information (the key!)
3. RSA encryption is modular exponentiation of a message with an exponent e and a modulus NN which is normally a product of two primes: N=p⋅q
4. Together, the exponent and modulus form an RSA "public key" The most common value for e is 0x10001 or 6553765537
5. If the prime factors of the product N can be deduced, you can calculate the Euler's totient of N to assist in getting the key to decrypt the message.
6. By taking the modular multiplicative inverse of the exponent e (e squared by -1) modulo ϕ(N), you can discover the private key to decrypt the message
6a. use pow(e, -1, modulus) to do this!
